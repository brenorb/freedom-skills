# Field Kit Checklist

Build for the actual mission, environment, people, and exit plan.

## Workflow

1. Define duration, terrain, weather, connectivity, power, team size, medical
   needs, legal restrictions, and what information must not be exposed.
2. Select minimum devices, chargers, spare power, offline maps, contact cards,
   secure storage, documentation tools, first-aid/safety items, and printed
   instructions.
3. Verify updates, backups, device locks, emergency contacts, offline content,
   cable compatibility, and safe separation of personal and field data.
4. Assign kit ownership, check-in schedule, lost-device response, and return or
   destruction plan for sensitive material.
5. Run a short rehearsal with the actual devices and mark items as packed,
   tested, expendable, or not safe to carry.

Do not give tactical or legal assurances beyond the user's location and expert
guidance. Avoid carrying unnecessary identifying data.

## Minimal check

Every critical item has a tested substitute or an explicit failure response.
