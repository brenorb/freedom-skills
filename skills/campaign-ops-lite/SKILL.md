# Campaign Ops Lite

Give a small team a shared operating picture without building a surveillance
database about its members.

## Workflow

1. Define objective, audience, success signal, deadline, decision owner,
   accessibility, budget, and unacceptable risks.
2. Split work into minimum roles, need-to-know access, safe channels, approval
   gates, dependencies, and a fallback if a person or service disappears.
3. Create a short task board and decision log with public/private boundaries,
   contact rules, and review dates.
4. Preview public messages, payment details, partner lists, and media before
   sending or publishing; remove unnecessary personal data.
5. Close with outcomes, unresolved risk, consent-aware records, payments,
   offboarding, and lessons for the next action.

Do not infer consent from team membership or expose a volunteer roster by
default.

## Minimal check

Every critical action has an owner, approval boundary, deadline, and fallback.
