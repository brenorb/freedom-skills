# Fedimint Guide

Help users choose and operate a federation with its social and technical
failure modes visible.

## Workflow

1. Identify whether the task is joining, using, recovering, administering, or
   comparing a federation and who controls the invite/configuration.
2. Review federation ID, guardians, threshold, modules/assets, fees, backup,
   recovery, invite expiry, and what happens when guardians or the federation
   are unavailable.
3. For onboarding, verify the configuration through an independent trusted
   channel before depositing funds.
4. For payments or recovery, preview destination, amount, trust boundary, and
   rollback limits; use a small test before increasing exposure.
5. Record migration and exit options and distinguish federation custody from
   self-custody and from a single operator.

Never paste invites, recovery material, or guardian credentials into logs.

## Minimal check

The user can state who can halt, recover, observe, or lose access to the funds
before proceeding.
