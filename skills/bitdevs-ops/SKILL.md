# Bitdevs Ops

Turn a topic list into a focused, source-linked technical conversation.

## Workflow

1. Set date, venue/online channel, audience, language, capacity, accessibility,
   privacy expectations, and organizer roles.
2. Gather primary readings and proposals; label each as discussion, demo,
   announcement, or unresolved question.
3. Draft an agenda with time boxes, moderation rules, speaker consent, and a
   no-recording or attribution policy where needed.
4. Prepare the public announcement without exposing private attendee or source
   information; confirm every external send.
5. After the event, record decisions, unanswered questions, links, and follow-up
   owners. Pay or reimburse only with explicit approval and correct details.

Do not publish private attendee lists or imply endorsement from attendance.

## Minimal check

The agenda has a source for each technical topic, an owner for each action, and
an explicit recording/attribution policy.
