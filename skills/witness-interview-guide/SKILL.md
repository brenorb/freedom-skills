# Witness Interview Guide

Help an interviewer gather a reliable account without leading, pressuring, or
creating unnecessary risk for the witness.

## Workflow

1. Confirm identity, safe channel, interpreter needs, consent to record, and
   the witness's right to pause, correct, or withdraw.
2. Explain purpose, audience, retention, publication choices, and limits of
   confidentiality in plain language.
3. Start with open prompts, then clarify time, place, sequence, source of
   knowledge, direct observations, and uncertainty. Separate fact from memory
   or hearsay.
4. Avoid leading questions, repeated pressure, promises, and unnecessary
   demographic or location details. Record the witness's own corrections.
5. End with safety check, preferred follow-up, consent scope, and a review
   opportunity. Store recording and notes under separate access controls.

This is not a substitute for trauma-informed practice, legal advice, or an
independent investigator.

## Minimal check

The notes identify what was directly observed, what was inferred, what remains
uncertain, and what the witness consented to share.
