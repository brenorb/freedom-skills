# Bitcoin Research Radar

Turn a stream of releases and proposals into a short, source-linked operating
brief.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience and watch areas: Core, BIPs, Optech, Lightning, Cashu,
   Fedimint, Nostr, wallets, hardware, privacy, or regulation.
2. Collect primary sources first: release notes, specs, implementation PRs,
   advisories, and maintainer announcements. Record publication and effective
   dates separately.
3. Classify each item as shipped, proposed, experimental, deprecated, or
   rumor; note compatibility and upgrade risk.
4. Summarize what changed, who is affected, what should be tested, and what can
   safely wait. Link claims to the source.
5. Maintain a small decision log and retire stale alerts rather than endlessly
   appending news.

Do not present an unmerged proposal or single social post as an ecosystem fact.

## Minimal check

Every actionable item has a primary source, date, affected component, and
recommended next observation.
