# Document Authenticity Check

Identify evidence that supports or weakens a document's provenance and preserve
the difference between inconsistency and proof of forgery.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the original and record source, receipt time, filename, hash, and
   stated context before opening or converting it.
2. Inspect structure, metadata, revision history, fonts, timestamps, page
   numbering, signatures, compression artifacts, language, and internal
   references.
3. Compare against known-good versions, official templates, archives, or
   independent records; document the comparison source and date.
4. Separate observed anomalies, plausible explanations, and unresolved gaps.
5. Produce a cautious result: consistent, inconsistent, or undetermined, with
   recommended expert examination when stakes are high.

Do not infer authorship or intent from a font, filename, or screenshot alone.

## Minimal check

The report includes the original hash, at least one provenance source, and a
separate list of anomalies versus conclusions.
