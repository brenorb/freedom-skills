# Press Room Builder

Make it easy for a journalist to verify and use the right material without
exposing sources or publishing stale claims.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, spokesperson, publication date, languages, contact channel,
   attribution rules, embargo, and source-consent boundary.
2. Assemble a short fact sheet, chronology, key documents, approved images,
   captions, credits, hashes/archives, and contact instructions.
3. Separate verified facts, allegations, context, unknowns, and quotes; add
   “last checked” dates and correction ownership.
4. Scrub metadata and private source information from public derivatives; keep
   original evidence access-controlled.
5. Test the static room on a clean device, provide low-bandwidth/downloadable
   copies, and publish only after the owner approves the exact content.

Do not promise source confidentiality or let a press room become a searchable
archive of unnecessary personal data.

## Minimal check

Every claim and asset has a source/credit, consent status, freshness date, and
correction contact.
