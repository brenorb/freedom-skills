# Evidence Priority Sorter

Make review order explicit without treating priority as truth.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, deadline, audience, and harm if an item is missed or
   exposed.
2. Score or label each item for directness, corroboration, integrity,
   uniqueness, urgency, consent, privacy risk, and publication readiness.
3. Group duplicates and derivatives while retaining the strongest original and
   all provenance links.
4. Separate “review first,” “preserve first,” “publishable,” “needs consent,”
   and “do not distribute” queues.
5. Revisit scores when new evidence, source correction, or safety information
   changes the context.

Do not equate vivid media, large files, or high emotional impact with evidentiary
strength.

## Minimal check

Each top-priority item has a reason, source/provenance pointer, and stated
privacy or consent status.
