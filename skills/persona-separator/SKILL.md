# Persona Separator

Help a user understand linkability before they reuse an identity across
contexts.

## Workflow

1. Name the personas and the consequence of accidental linkage; do not ask for
   legal identity when it is not needed.
2. Compare handles, avatars, bios, domains, recovery details, contact graphs,
   writing patterns, timezone, posting cadence, and reused media.
3. Mark each connection as intentional, possible, or prohibited, and prioritize
   high-confidence links that can be changed.
4. Propose minimal corrections: unique aliases, separate profiles, metadata
   cleaning, contact import disabled, or content review before publishing.
5. Re-check the public view from a clean session and document residual risks.

Do not attempt to deanonymize another person or claim that a new alias erases
historical linkage.

## Minimal check

The result lists known intentional links separately from inferred or uncertain
connections.
