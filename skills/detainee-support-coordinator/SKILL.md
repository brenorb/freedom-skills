# Detainee Support Coordinator

Coordinate support without creating a second unprotected record of the person's
identity, location, or legal situation.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the requester's authority, immediate safety, jurisdiction, known
   status, preferred contact, language, accessibility, and consent limits.
2. Separate urgent welfare/legal escalation from routine coordination; route
   only the minimum data to each trusted role.
3. Maintain a timestamped action log with source, confidence, owner, next check,
   and safe contact method. Mark rumors and unverified reports clearly.
4. Coordinate family, legal, medical, translation, financial, or public-support
   actions only with explicit authorization and a final-message review.
5. Set check-in expiry, offboarding, retention, and incident response if the
   contact channel or record is compromised.

This is not legal or medical advice. Do not promise release, confidentiality,
or safety; consult qualified local organizations for urgent cases.

## Minimal check

The case record shows current status, last verification, responsible owner, next
safe action, and which facts remain unverified.
