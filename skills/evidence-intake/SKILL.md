# Evidence Intake

Capture enough context to make later review possible while collecting the least
personal data necessary.

## Workflow

1. Confirm consent, urgency, safe contact method, retention expectations, and
   who is authorized to access the case.
2. Assign a case identifier; record received time, source-provided context,
   original filenames, media types, and any immediate safety concern.
3. Copy originals to read-only or clearly preserved storage. Do not edit,
   rename away, transcode, or strip metadata from the original evidence.
4. Create working copies for transcription, translation, redaction, and
   analysis; link every derivative to its source hash.
5. Mark unknowns, contradictions, consent gaps, and required follow-up instead
   of filling them with assumptions.

Do not promise confidentiality beyond the actual storage and access controls.
Do not collect unrelated identity or location details.

## Minimal check

Every intake record has a case ID, received time, source hash or reason it is
missing, consent status, and next safe action.
