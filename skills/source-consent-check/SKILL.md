# Source Consent Check

Turn vague permission into a concrete, revocable sharing boundary.

## Workflow

1. Identify the source, material, purpose, audience, retention period, and
   people or risks the source is trying to avoid.
2. Ask whether consent was informed, voluntary, specific, and appropriate to
   the current use; distinguish permission to receive from permission to
   publish.
3. Record limits: anonymization, delayed publication, region exclusion,
   redaction, named reviewers, expiration, and withdrawal route.
4. Check whether a child, coercive situation, emergency, or third-party data
   requires a higher protection threshold or specialist advice.
5. If consent is missing or ambiguous, pause publication and propose the least
   exposing alternative.

Do not treat silence, a public post, or a previous unrelated permission as
blanket consent.

## Minimal check

The final record states what was authorized, for whom, for what purpose, and
what remains prohibited or uncertain.
