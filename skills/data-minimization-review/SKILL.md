# Data Minimization Review

Reduce collection and exposure without breaking the legitimate task.

## Workflow

1. State the purpose and decisions the data must support; inventory every field,
   derived value, log, export, backup, and recipient.
2. Mark each item required, useful, optional, duplicate, risky, or unjustified;
   identify sensitive combinations that create new exposure.
3. Recommend lower-precision alternatives: categories, ranges, pseudonymous
   IDs, local processing, shorter retention, or role-limited views.
4. Check access, sync, analytics, notifications, vendor sharing, deletion, and
   recovery behavior across the whole lifecycle.
5. Produce a change list with owner, rationale, verification, and an exception
   process for genuinely necessary collection.

Do not delete material needed for safety, consent, accountability, or legal
obligations without the responsible owner's decision.

## Minimal check

Every retained field has a purpose, owner, access boundary, and retention/deletion
rule.
