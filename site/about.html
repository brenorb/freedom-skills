About

From raw tools to human rights.

Every freedom tool comes with its own manual. Freedom Skills lets your agent read them for you. Here’s how, in five steps.

The five chapters

  1. Raw tools
  2. Tested skills
  3. Guided use
  4. Safer action
  5. Human rights

01Raw tools

Good tools, each with its own homework.

Free, open-source tools already let you message privately, pay without a bank, publish without a host and keep records nobody can quietly change. But each one comes with its own interface, setup and security homework, so most people settle for a mainstream app that pays its bills with their data.

02Tested skills

The manual, rewritten for an agent.

A skill is a plain-text SKILL.md that writes down how a real tool is used: the commands, their order, what to check and what tends to go wrong. Your agent follows it instead of guessing, in any agent that supports the open Agent Skills standard (opens in a new tab).

03Guided use

You say what you want. The agent does the reading.

You ask in plain words, like Send the signed PDF to Ana on Signal. Your agent picks the tested skill, uses the real tool and shows you the caveats before anything happens, loading a skill’s full instructions only when your request needs them.

Watch a run →

04Safer action

It stops before anything you can’t undo.

Nothing is sent, paid or published until you say yes. Every skill is plain text you can read, or scan with SkillSpector, before installing; Rampart hooks keep selected personal details from reaching the model; and where money is involved, your keys stay with you.

05Human rights

Who it’s for.

When an agent can run these tools carefully, communicating, paying and organising with autonomy stops being a privilege of experts, for developers, researchers and people in sensitive situations alike. Freedom Skills is open source: propose a skill, improve a workflow or report what’s broken.
